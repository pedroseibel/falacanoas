# FalaCanoas: name, identity and imagery research

Research date: 2026-10-12. All URLs were opened or queried on that date unless marked otherwise. Statements are labelled **verified** (read at the source), **secondary** (read in a search summary, guide site or Wikipedia) or **unverified**. Ideas are only in section 5 and are labelled as ideas.

Sibling research that this file does not repeat: `../canoas/README.md` (city facts, flood numbers, complaint channels), `../identity/README.md`, `../prior-art/README.md`.

## TL;DR

1. **No existing "Fala Canoas", "FalaCanoas" or "Fala, Canoas" entity turned up**, but my search tools ignored exact-phrase and `site:` operators, so absence is *not proven*. Still to do by hand: INPI trademark search, Instagram/Facebook search while logged in, Google with quotes.
2. **The real naming risk is an official lookalike.** Canoas's own ombudsman is literally "Ouvidoria Municipal – Fala.BR" (the federal platform), and the pattern exists as Fala SP and Fala Brasil (Record TV). The Prefeitura's own newsletter has used a "Fala, PREFEITO!" section (Jornal da Prefeitura, Jan 2019). "Fala Canoas" will be read as official unless independence is stated on first contact.
3. **Domains looked free on 2026-10-12.** RDAP returned 404 (no registration record) for falacanoas and fala-canoas on .com.br, .org.br, .net.br, .com, .org, .net and .app. That is a snapshot, not a reservation; register soon if the name is chosen.
4. **Handles:** GitHub, YouTube, Bluesky look unclaimed; Telegram and TikTok probably. Instagram, Facebook, X, Threads cannot be judged without logging in.
5. **Canoas identity:** the name comes from canoes built by the Sinos/Gravataí (the 1874 railway stop was "Capão das Canoas"); emancipation 27 Jun 1939; motto "Progresso"; tree symbol the timbaúva; brasão has three golden canoes, a 16-tooth cogwheel and a factory; flag is green and red with a yellow cogwheel.
6. **Avoid what reads as Prefeitura:** brasão, the green/red/yellow flag palette, "Progresso", gov.br blue and Rawline, and words like Ouvidoria, Protocolo, Canal oficial. Never use alert styling: a false evacuation alert went out in Mathias Velho on 26 May 2024.
7. **Agência Brasil is not usable.** Its site no longer says CC BY 3.0 BR; reproduction is authorised for journalism with credit and commercial use needs a licence. Wikimedia Commons is the real source.
8. **Imagery:** 34 candidates tabled, 15 downloaded to `photos/` (all at least 2048 px wide, 11 at least 3000 px) with `CREDITS.md`. Best: the river-gauge sign by a Gravataí creek, the CC0 post-flood Rio Branco streets (use sparingly), Trensurb over the BR-116, the Antiga Estação, a bus shelter in the rain.
9. **Gaps and traps:** Commons has no usable photo of Parque Getúlio Vargas, REFAP, Ilha das Garças or any Canoas pothole. The "Panorâmicos de Canoas" set in Category:Canoas is not Canoas, RS. "Catedral São Luís Gonzaga" is not confirmed; sources say Igreja Matriz. Governo RS and Lula Oficial photo sets are built around politicians.
10. **Tone:** peers pair a plain promise with an explicit independence line (Meu Rio: "não aceitamos dinheiro de governos, partidos, empresas ou bancos"). Colab is the counter-model: it works *for* prefeituras.
11. **Ideas (section 5):** 8 wordmark treatments and 3 brand directions: "A régua", "Canoa e correnteza", "A vizinhança fala" (bus-shelter orange).

**Reading of the three spellings (opinion):**
- "Fala, Canoas" (with comma) is a vocative: both "Speak up, Canoas" and the colloquial "Fala, [name]!" greeting. It is the warmest and most clearly not a government label.
- "Fala Canoas" (no comma) is closest to "Fala.BR" / "Fala SP" and therefore the most official-looking.
- "FalaCanoas" (one word) works as a domain and handle; as the display name it is flat but unambiguous.

### 2.2 Brasão and flag

- **Brasão** (Lei 824, 10 Dec 1963; designed by João Palma da Silva): Portuguese shield. Upper part: sky blue, light-green hills, blue water for three rivers (Caí, Sinos, Gravataí) carrying three golden canoes. Lower part: bottle-green field with a 16-tooth golden wheel for industry, charged with a factory with three chimneys. Above: a winged golden helmet for commerce, and a silver mural crown with five towers for the city. Below: a ribbon in the Rio Grande do Sul tricolour with the name and two dates. Source: [FOTW/Flagspot Canoas](https://www.fotw.info/FLAGS/br-rs080.html). The same page prints the founding date as "27 July 1939", which conflicts with every other source (27 June); treat it as a typo.
- **Flag** (adopted between 24 Nov 1978 and 5 Jul 1979 per FOTW): diagonally divided green and red, with the brasão on a white disc ringed by a yellow cogwheel. Wikipedia's reading of the colours: green and yellow for Brazil, red for blood shed "em nome da liberdade do Rio Grande do Sul". Image files with public-domain tags exist on Commons ([Category:Symbols of Canoas](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Symbols_of_Canoas)), but see the avoid list below.
- The Prefeitura's own sign style (seen on a 2011 photo of the Centro Social Urbano Mathias Velho) pairs the brasão with a green/yellow/red diagonal stripe and "PREFEITURA DE CANOAS". The current logotype was **not** verified from a primary source.

### 2.4 Flora and fauna
- **Timbaúva**, the tree symbol and, by the name legend, the tree the first canoes were cut from.
- **Garças** (egrets) give the Ilha das Garças its name. The Delta do Jacuí protected area reportedly has almost 200 bird species (search summary of an O Eco PDF; secondary). The state park and APA cover islands, swamps and flooded fields shared with Porto Alegre and other municipalities.
- **Parque Getúlio Vargas** shelters rehabilitated animals in a small zoo (guide sites, secondary).
- Not wildlife but unavoidable: **Caramelo**, the horse found on a roof in Canoas in May 2024, four days stranded, treated at the Ulbra hospital and adopted by Ulbra; he became a symbol of the flood in Rio Grande do Sul ([Band Agro](https://www.band.com.br/agro/noticias/cavalo-caramelo-que-foi-resgatado-de-telhado-vira-celebridade-na-expointer-202408301214), [DOL](https://dol.com.br/noticias/brasil/904318/caramelo-cavalo-simbolo-da-enchente-no-rs-ganha-nova-vida); summarised from search results, articles not opened one by one). A Commons photo exists ("Cavalo Caramelo, na ULBRA em 2025"). He is a beloved image, but also the property of a third party and a marker of a painful week.

### 2.5 The 2024 flood as visual memory
Numbers are in `../canoas/README.md`; this is about pictures.
- **What the public image of the flood in Canoas looks like:** aerial views of brown water over Rio Branco and Mathias Velho; rescue boats in streets; shelters; then, for months, mountains of ruined furniture on the pavement, mud lines at waist height on walls, hand-written signs on doors and piles, the horse on the roof.
- **What is on Commons under open licences** ([Category:2024 Canoas floods](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:2024_Canoas_floods), 68 files): 36 aerial photos from the 5 May presidential overflight (Lula Oficial / Ricardo Stuckert), 12 street-level photos by the Defensoria Pública da União in Rio Branco on 16 Jun 2024 (CC0), Marinha "Operação Abrigo pelo Mar" photos, damage maps (CC BY 4.0).
- **Trust lesson:** on 26 May 2024 soldiers in Mathias Velho gave a false evacuation alert after unconfirmed reports of a dike rupture; about 11,900 people went to shelters in Canoas and the Army removed those involved and called it a grave procedural error ([O Imparcial](https://oimparcial.com.br/noticias/2024/05/enchentes-militares-comunicam-falso-alerta-de-evacuacao-no-rs-e-sao-afastados/), read once). FalaCanoas must never resemble an alert system: no sirens, red "ALERTA" banners, urgent push wording, or "official" language.
- **Handling rules (recommended):**
  - Never use flood imagery as the hero or in the sign-up flow. One calm, people-free image, in a context block, with date and credit.
  - Prefer infrastructure and trace over victims: the river-gauge sign, a debris-lined street, a mud line. No faces, no children, no interiors of homes.
  - No aerial "disaster tourism" shots, and none with the president or governor (they carry political branding and, in 2026, an election context).
  - Ask residents of Rio Branco and Mathias Velho what they want to see before publishing; many lost their homes.
  - Consider a typographic flood motif (a waterline) instead of a photograph.

### 2.6 What would read as "official Prefeitura" (avoid)
- **Symbols:** the brasão; the flag or its green/red/yellow diagonal; the cogwheel and three-canoes combination; the Prefeitura tricolour stripe; the motto "Progresso"; the "Voa Canoas" 70-anos totem (likely municipal) and other commemorative marks.
- **Palette and type:** gov.br blue (#1351B4) and the Rawline typeface (the official gov.br design-system face, so using it would imitate government); the national green, yellow and blue as a trio.
- **Words:** Ouvidoria, Protocolo, Canal oficial, Atendimento ao cidadão, Central, CAC, 156, SIC, "Fala.BR"; "Prefeitura" + anything.
- **Visuals:** photos of Prefeitura events (banners, politicians), Trensurb events that include "PREFEITURA DE CANOAS" banners, flags in frame.
- **Channels:** a WhatsApp number or domain resembling the CAC or the Zap da Prefeitura; any .gov.br or canoas.rs.gov.br subdomain; alert-style push notifications.
- **Voice:** passive, bureaucratic phrasing ("Sua manifestação foi registrada"), and promises of resolution that only the Prefeitura can keep.

**What the references suggest for FalaCanoas** (observations, then recommendations):
- The headline names the thing and the place in one plain sentence (FixMyStreet, Meu Rio, Colab). Verbs carry the page (Reclame Aqui: "Pesquise. Reclame. Resolva").
- Independence is said as a **refusal or a fact**, never as an adjective: "não aceitamos dinheiro de…", "nunca vendemos", "Built by mySociety, a registered charity". FalaCanoas can do the same with true statements: no Prefeitura money, no party, open source, who the volunteers are.
- Credibility comes from counters and named wins. Before launch there are none, so use method and transparency: what the scoreboard counts, where the data comes from, who runs it. Do not borrow Colab-style "80% resolved" claims; self-reported rates need a label.
- Sign-up CTAs are low-commitment and benefit-led ("receba nossos alertas"; Rede Nossa SP: "preencha o formulário"). For a by-bairro waitlist the natural first field is the bairro, the way FixMyStreet's first field is the street.
- Avoid the two failure modes: the prefeitura-integrated voice (Colab: "problemas para a prefeitura") and the campaign voice (Nossas), which would break the non-partisan promise.

## 5. Ideas (not findings)

Everything below is an idea for discussion, rooted in the findings above. None is verified or tested.

### 5.1 Wordmark treatments (8)

1. **The comma as canoe.** Set "Fala, Canoas" with a large, soft-serif comma whose swash reads as a canoe hull seen from the side. Rewards the vocative spelling and puts the origin story in a single glyph.
2. **Camel-case with a waterline.** "FalaCanoas" with the capital C as an open crescent (a hull) and a thin horizontal waterline under the whole word. Works as an avatar (just "FC" plus the waterline).
3. **Gauge ticks.** A baseline of tiny régua fluviométrica tick marks under or inside the word, echoing the sign in photo 01. The ticks can later become the "days open" scale of the scoreboard, so the logo and the product are the same object.
4. **Stacked caps, façade lettering.** "FALA / CANOAS" in the proportions of the capital lettering on the Antiga Estação façade and the Villa Mimosa sign. Local, dignified. Risk: station-sign lettering plus a blue band would look like Trensurb or a Prefeitura plaque; keep it off blue.
5. **Pin-bubble hybrid (the obvious one).** A map pin whose tail is a speech-bubble tail. Instantly legible, and also the most generic civic-app mark. A fallback, not a lead.
6. **Lowercase "falacanoas" with a pin for the "o".** Friendly and app-like for avatars and URLs; the "o" is a filled circle. Risk: too small-company, low gravity for a scoreboard that criticises slow repairs.
7. **Wake lines.** "Fala, Canoas!" with two or three thin ripple lines trailing behind the word like the wake of a canoe. Warm, social-media friendly. Risk: exclamation mark pushes toward campaign tone.
8. **Heritage drop capitals.** Art Nouveau-flavoured F and C in the Villa Mimosa spirit, with the rest of the word in a plain grotesque. Distinctive; needs a licensed or self-drawn alphabet, since the source is a photographed facade, not a font.

### 5.2 Three brand directions in words

**A. "A régua" (the gauge).** The product measures: how long an issue has stayed open. Visual world: gauge signs, kerbs, rulers, tabular numbers, a thin red-yellow-green band used only as a data scale and never as decoration (it collides with the official flag colours, so limit it to small chart marks, never a header). Photography: wires, kerbs, bus shelters, the Gravataí gauge, the Antiga Estação. Voice: factual, plain, a little dry ("Aberto há 43 dias"). Strength: matches the scoreboard and the "proof over promise" principle of the sibling gov-br project. Risk: cold; needs a human sign-off line.

**B. "Canoa e correnteza" (canoe and current).** The name story is the identity: three canoes on three rivers (what the brasão already says, so do not copy the heraldry; take the idea only), timbaúva bark, river light. Visual world: hand-drawn or woodcut wake lines, a river palette (silt, reed green, slate, warm paper), a serif with some warmth. Photography: river edges, fishers, Trensurb over the BR-116. Voice: patient, observant, local. Strength: unmistakably Canoas and not generic civic-tech. Risk: nostalgia and tourism-brochure feel; the river is also where the 2024 trauma comes from, so the flood must not be romanticised.

**C. "A vizinhança fala" (the neighbourhood speaks).** Bairro-first. A grid of bairro tiles, big quotes in colloquial pt-BR set in large type, ordinary-street photography (shelters, corner houses, wires). One signal colour: the orange of the bus shelter in photo 11 (an orange is outside both the official green/yellow/red and gov.br blue, and it reads "something here needs attention"). Voice: "fala, vizinho" energy, never officialese. Strength: fits the first persona (an engaged neighbour already in bairro groups) and the sign-up by bairro. Risk: orange is also a safety-cone and a Colab-adjacent warmth; test it against the competing apps and make sure the tone does not slide into a political rally.

**Shared guardrails** (from sections 1 and 2): state independence beside the name; no brasão, no flag palette, no gov.br blue; no Rawline (the gov.br typeface); keep the gov-br concept's polish bar (a display serif such as Fraunces, careful tracking, credited real photographs, footnoted claims) but pair it with a different text face; never use alert styling.
